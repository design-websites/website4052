Frequently Asked Questions

How many outlets do I need in each room? ▼

The National Electrical Code recommends an outlet every 6 feet of wall space. Modern best practice suggests even more, especially in kitchens and home offices. We can assess your needs and recommend the optimal number.

Do I need GFCI outlets in my bathroom? ▼

Yes, by code all bathroom outlets within 6 feet of a sink must be GFCI protected. GFCI outlets prevent electrical shocks in wet environments. We ensure your bathroom is code-compliant and safe.

Can I add an outlet anywhere in my house? ▼

Most locations work, but we assess factors like existing wiring, circuit capacity, and your electrical panel capacity. Some locations may require wiring extensions, new circuits, or even panel upgrades. We'll discuss all options and costs upfront.

What's the difference between GFCI and AFCI outlets? ▼

GFCI (Ground Fault): Protects from electrical shock, required in wet areas like bathrooms and kitchens. AFCI (Arc Fault): Prevents electrical fires from faulty wiring, required in bedrooms and living areas. Both are important safety features.

Are smart outlets worth installing? ▼

Smart outlets offer convenience and energy savings. You can control devices remotely, schedule them to turn on/off automatically, and monitor energy usage. They're especially useful for entertainment systems, lighting, and appliances. Installation cost is slightly higher but the benefits often justify it.

How long does outlet installation take? ▼

A single outlet typically takes 1-2 hours. Multiple outlets on the same circuit can be done in a few hours. If new circuits or wiring are needed, it may take longer. We provide a timeline during your consultation.

Do you need permits for outlet installation? ▼

Most outlet installations require permits to ensure safety and code compliance. We handle all permitting and inspections for you. This protects your investment and ensures everything meets current electrical codes.

What warranty do you provide? ▼

All outlet installations come with a one-year warranty on workmanship. If any issues arise from our installation, we'll fix them at no cost. Most outlets also come with manufacturer warranties covering the hardware itself.
